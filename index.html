Kaiwei Wu

I am a Ph.D. student in solid mechanics at Peking University, supervised by Prof. Yin Zhang. My research investigates the multiscale mechanics of materials under coupled multiphysics loading.

I combine continuum and crystal-plasticity modeling, phase-field methods, experiments, and machine-learning interatomic potentials to study interface mechanics, phase transformations, nanotwinning, and dislocation structures.

Research Interests

  • Multiscale mechanics of materials
  • Coupled-field material behavior
  • Interface mechanics in solids
  • Phase transformations and nanotwinning
  • Dislocation cell evolution
  • Generalized continuum theories
  • Crystal plasticity modeling
  • Machine-learning interatomic potentials

My current work connects microscopic mechanisms to macroscopic response through theory, computation, and experiment.
